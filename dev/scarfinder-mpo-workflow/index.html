Constrained ScarFinder with layer MPOs

This page describes the numerical primitives for a ScarFinder search on a general χ = 2 iMPS in the staggered PXP chain. A complete example is examples/pxp_scarfinder_point.jl.

One iteration

U = trotter_mpos(layers, 0.05; n=2, gate_map=G -> P3 * G)
r = scarfinder_step!(ψ, U, 2; nstep=20, maxdim=16, cutoff=1e-12,
                     terms=layers, target=e, n=2)

The step runs evolve!, truncates each bond with truncate! and calls energy_fix! when given a target. The real-time gates carry the blockade projector. The correction uses ordinary imaginary-time gates built from layers. The next real-time window projects out the small leakage introduced after the previous window. Measure it separately with constraint_leakage(ψ, Blockade()).

The result is (loss, energy_error, converged). The loss is minus the log of the per-site fidelity between the evolved and truncated states. The energy error is the residual of the state after correction.

Recording trajectories

scarfinder!(ψ, U, χ, N; refine=false, observe=f, …) calls f(ψ, k, r) for k = 0 (initial state, r = nothing) and after each iteration with the returned candidate and its named tuple. ψ is the live state; copy it to keep it. Use refine=false whenever the caller selects its own representative: the default refine=true adds a minimum-entropy scan that can replace ψ. Refinement samples are neither observed nor in history.

evolve!(ψ, mpos, steps; observe=f, every=k) calls f(φ, s, stats) at s = 0, every k complete Trotter steps, and at the last step. φ is the state at time s·dt, identical to evolve!(copy, mpos, s) from the same input. At a merged half-step boundary the pending half step is applied to a snapshot; the main trajectory is unchanged. Splitting a window into single-step calls instead changes the integrator: at χ = 2, ten calls of evolve!(…, 1) differed from one ten-step window by 1.36e-5 in n_A. release_recorder records physical time, both bond entropies, retained dimensions, discarded weight, num_saturated (requested dimension exceeded the cap), num_at_cap (retained dimension equal to the cap), site occupations and the per-site return fidelity. How to turn these into a score is a project decision.

Reproducibility and symmetry

  • rand_iMPS(rng, T, n, d, dim) uses the same uniform rand distribution as rand_iMPS(T, n, d, dim). Complex Gaussian seeds (randn) belong to a different distribution and are drawn directly. Derive one RNG per task from (grid point, seed id).
  • translate(ψ, k) shifts site tensors with their right-bond spectra and returns independent arrays. On the staggered chain a one-site translation maps H(μ, δ) to H(μ, −δ), so at δ ≠ 0 it is not a symmetry of a grid point. Complex conjugation of a real H reverses time.

Migration notes

  • per_site_fidelity returns the amplitude |⟨a|b⟩|^(1/n). A squared per-site overlap is its square, F_old = F_new²: a closure tolerance 1 − F_old < ε becomes 1 − F_new < 1 − √(1 − ε) ≈ ε/2, and a −log distance halves.
  • constraint_leakage is the per-site negative log of the legal amplitude, not the maximum double occupancy max⟨n_i n_{i+1}⟩; thresholds do not carry over.
  • Entropy (ent_S, natural log, per bond), "retained dimension reached the cap" and "requested dimension exceeded the cap" are separate measurements.
